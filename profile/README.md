## What we build

Quenvyra is a multi-agent RAG app that answers customer and product-review questions from your own orders, tickets, reviews and policies. The AI backend and a customizable React front end ship together in a single container.

- **Order and customer lookup:** exact search by email or order number, plus natural-language search across orders, tickets and knowledge articles.
- **Product review intelligence:** sizing trends, recurring defects and what customers actually say.
- **Ticket inbox with suggested replies:** drafted in the customer's language, reviewed by your team before sending.
- **Knowledge base manager and guardrail tester:** keep policies current and probe risky requests before shoppers see them.
- **Customer widget and admin console:** an embeddable shopper widget and a team console, both bundled.
- **Multilingual:** customer answers in the shopper's language, with ticket analysis in English, Spanish, French and German.
- **Tenant-isolated:** every row and vector carries the shop domain, and every query filters on it.

Built for headless storefronts such as Shopify Hydrogen, Remix, Next.js, WooCommerce and BigCommerce.

## Getting access

Quenvyra is commercial software distributed to licensed customers through GitHub.

1. **Purchase** a license at [quenvyra.app](https://www.quenvyra.app/) and receive your key by email.
2. **Redeem** the key on the access page with your GitHub username.
3. **Accept** the repository invitation, then pull the container image and download the front-end release.

Full setup steps are in the product README, which you will see after redemption.
